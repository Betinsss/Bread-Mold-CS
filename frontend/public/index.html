Best Storage: Airtight container or sealed bag

Storage Time: 3-4 days at room temperature

🦠 Mold Types

Mold Aspergillus

Definition: Black or dark-colored mold that can produce mycotoxins

Appearance: Black, brown, or greenish-black spots

Risk Level: Very High - potentially toxic

Health Impact: Can cause respiratory issues and allergic reactions

Mold Cladosporium

Definition: Olive-green to brown or black mold commonly found on bread

Appearance: Dark green to black velvety patches

Risk Level: Moderate to High

Health Impact: Can trigger allergies and asthma symptoms

Mold Penicillium

Definition: Blue-green mold, most common bread mold species

Appearance: Blue, green, or white fuzzy growth

Risk Level: High - spreads rapidly

Health Impact: Generally not toxic but can cause allergic reactions

Mold Rhizopus

Definition: Black bread mold that grows quickly in warm conditions

Appearance: White fuzzy growth that turns black with spores

Risk Level: High - rapid growth

Health Impact: Can cause infections in immunocompromised individuals

📅 Bread Age Classification

Fresh (1 day)

Characteristics: No mold, optimal quality and texture, soft and moist

Storage Time: 0-3 days

Recommendation: Safe to consume, store properly to maintain freshness

Slightly Aged (4 days)

Characteristics: Minor staleness, minimal mold risk, slightly firmer texture

Storage Time: 3-5 days

Recommendation: Still safe to eat, consider toasting for better texture

Old (6 days)

Characteristics: Noticeable staleness, mold likely present, dry texture

Storage Time: 5-7 days

Recommendation: Inspect carefully for mold before consuming

Very Old (10+ days)

Characteristics: Significant deterioration, high mold risk, hard and dry

Storage Time: 7+ days

Recommendation: Not recommended for consumption, likely contaminated

Medium-sized YOLOv11 model trained specifically for bread and mold detection with high accuracy and real-time performance. The model achieves excellent precision in identifying both bread types and mold contamination, making it reliable for food safety assessment.

📊 Test Set Performance

The model was evaluated on a comprehensive test set with diverse bread types and mold conditions.

  • mAP@50: 95.8% - Mean Average Precision at 50% IoU threshold
  • Precision: 98.6% - Accuracy of positive predictions
  • Recall: 94.9% - Ability to find all positive instances

🎯 Model Capabilities

  • Detects 6 bread types: Flat, Quick, Stuffed, Sweet, White, Wholegrain
  • Identifies 4 mold types: Aspergillus, Cladosporium, Penicillium, Rhizopus
  • Real-time detection with bounding box visualization
  • Calculates mold coverage percentage accurately
  • Estimates bread age and storage time

⚙️ Technical Specifications

  • Architecture: YOLOv11 Medium
  • Framework: Roboflow API
  • Input: RGB images (any resolution)
  • Confidence Threshold: 25%
  • Detection Method: Object detection with bounding boxes

🔬 Training Details

  • Trained on diverse dataset of bread and mold images
  • Includes various lighting conditions and angles
  • Augmented with rotation, scaling, and color variations
  • Optimized for both accuracy and inference speed
  • Regular updates with new data for improved performance
